How to read this, and where it comes from

Timeline events

    Metrics

    1-year change: this year's ADM versus last year's, as a percent. Catches year-to-year swings, including the pandemic year.

    3-year average annual change: the mean of the last three 1-year changes. Smooths single-year noise so persistent trends stand out.

    Cumulative change since base year: this year's ADM versus the base year's. The long-horizon view. Units are hatched for years before the base.

    Below own peak: this year's ADM versus the unit's highest ADM in any year up to and including this one. Zero means the unit is at its all-time high; every value is zero or negative.

    Charter share (county level): charter ADM located in the county divided by district plus charter ADM. Charter students are assigned to the county where the school sits, not where they live.

    Data

    Enrollment is Average Daily Membership (ADM) from NC DPI's School Report Card researcher data set, table rcd_adm (2024-25 release), for 2005-06 through 2024-25. The year label is the school year's ending year: "2024-25" is the 2025 row. Names and counties come from table rcd_location in the same release. Boundaries are U.S. Census Bureau 2023 cartographic boundary files (unified school districts and counties, 1:500k), applied to all years.

    The county view sums the districts in each county (Orange County is Orange County Schools plus Chapel Hill-Carrboro City Schools) and, when "District + charter" is on, adds charter schools located there. NC's two statewide virtual charters are counted in the statewide charter total only.

    Limits

    ADM is a state funding measure, not a headcount of resident children. Falling ADM can reflect fewer children, families choosing charter, private, or home schooling, or boundary changes. Private school and homeschool enrollment are on tab 2; the voucher events here mark policy timing, not measured transfers.

    Boundary change. Nash-Rocky Mount Schools was split along the county line for 2024-25 (Senate Bill 248): about 1,700 students on the Edgecombe side of Rocky Mount moved to Edgecombe County Public Schools, and the Nash district was renamed Nash County Public Schools. The 2024-25 drop for Nash (about 8%) and rise for Edgecombe (about 20%) are that transfer, not families moving. The map still draws the 2023 Census boundary, which is the pre-split shape.

    Districts that cross county lines. City districts such as Kannapolis (Cabarrus and Rowan) are counted in the county of their central office, so county totals are approximate where a district straddles a line. Three federal and tribal districts (Fort Bragg, Camp Lejeune, Cherokee Central) are not NC LEAs and are not shown, which lowers county totals in Cumberland, Onslow, Swain and Jackson. Two residential Schools for the Deaf are excluded.

    Pre-K is not in ADM, for any district or year: DPI counts K-12 membership only, and its separate pre-K table lists schools whose pre-K count exceeds their entire ADM. 2020-21 ADM was measured during remote and hybrid instruction; treat that year's one-year changes as a pandemic effect, not a trend. 2024-25: DPI notes that the move to a new student information system may have produced higher-than-expected final ADM counts. A full list of caveats is in LIMITATIONS.md next to this file.
